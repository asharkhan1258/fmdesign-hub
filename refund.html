Refund and Return Policy

At FM Design Hub, we value our customers and aim to provide high-quality products and services. Please read our Refund and Return Policy carefully before making a purchase.

1. Digital Products

  • All digital products such as graphics, templates, and downloadable files are non-refundable once the files have been delivered or downloaded.
  • If you experience technical issues accessing your purchase, please contact us within 7 days of purchase. We will work with you to resolve the problem.

2. Physical Products (if applicable)

  • If you receive a damaged or defective item, you must notify us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and proof of the issue.
  • Returns will only be accepted if the item is unused, in its original packaging, and returned within 14 days after approval.
  • Shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the customer, unless the return is due to our error.

3. Custom Orders

  • All custom design services are non-refundable once work has begun, as these are tailored specifically to your requirements.
  • If the project cannot be completed due to our fault, you will be eligible for a partial or full refund, depending on the work completed.

4. Order Cancellations

  • Orders for digital products cannot be cancelled once payment has been processed.
  • Orders for physical products may be cancelled within 24 hours of purchase, provided they have not been shipped.

5. How to Request a Refund or Return

Email us at contact@FMDesignHub.com with:

  • Your order number
  • Reason for refund or return
  • Relevant proof, if applicable

We will review your request and respond within 2–5 business days.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at:

contact@FMDesignHub.com
